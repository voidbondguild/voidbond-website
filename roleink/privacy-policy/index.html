General Information

RoleInk allows users to create and manage character-sheet templates and characters locally on their devices and to share them during local game sessions.

Devices communicate via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). The host selects a template and determines which connected clients receive it. Clients can create a character from the received template or select an existing character that matches the session template.

Character data is shared with the host only after the client explicitly selects “Send to host”. After this initial release, further saved changes to the selected character are transmitted automatically while sharing remains active in the current session. The host can view all attributes of the shared character. Switching to another character requires a new explicit release.

On certain older Android versions, location permission may be technically required by the operating system to scan for or connect to BLE devices. RoleInk does not use location information for tracking or determining your geographical position.

RoleInk is distributed through Google Play. Google may independently process information in connection with Google Play, Android and related services under its own privacy policies. This processing is separate from the local character-sheet and session functionality described here.

Data We Process

Depending on how you use RoleInk, the app processes the following data:

  • Client names: Names entered when joining a session.
  • Templates: Template titles, field labels, field types, default values, selection options and other field settings.
  • Characters: Character names and personal character attribute values entered or selected by users.
  • Technical identifiers: Bluetooth identifiers, a locally stored client identifier, template and character identifiers, and session and transfer identifiers.
  • Local management metadata: Template origin, use and dispatch records, and associated timestamps.
  • Temporary session information: Participant assignments, connection and transfer status, and character data received by the host.

RoleInk is intended for fictional character and game information. Real names are not required. Users are responsible for the content they enter and choose to share and should respect the privacy and rights of others.

Purpose of Processing

RoleInk processes this data to:

  • Create, display, edit and locally store templates and characters.
  • Establish and maintain BLE connections between session participants.
  • Send templates to clients selected by the host.
  • Share released character data and subsequent saved changes with the host.
  • Display the host’s read-only character overview and connection status.
  • Check that transferred characters match the current session and template.
  • Maintain template protection rules and prevent unintended changes to used or dispatched templates.

RoleInk does not include advertising and does not use app data for advertising.

Legal Basis

Personal data is processed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR to support our legitimate interests in providing local character-sheet and multiplayer functionality, reliable device communication and consistent template and character data, provided these interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.

Data Sharing

During a local game session:

  • The client name and technical information required for participation are exchanged with the host.
  • The host sends the session template only to the clients selected by the host.
  • After a client selects “Send to host”, the selected character’s name and all character attributes are made available to the host.
  • Further saved changes to that character are transmitted automatically while sharing remains active in the current session.

Storage Duration

Templates and characters remain stored locally after a session ends or the app is closed, until deleted by the user. Local identifiers and template management records are retained across sessions and may remain after individual templates or characters are deleted.

The host’s participant overview, received character data and sharing permissions are temporary and are cleared when the session ends or the host app’s process terminates.

Deleting data on your device does not delete copies already stored on other participants’ devices.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: Required to discover nearby session hosts, establish connections, host sessions and exchange session data.
  • Location permission on older Android versions: May be required by the operating system for BLE discovery. RoleInk does not use this permission to track users or determine their geographical position.
