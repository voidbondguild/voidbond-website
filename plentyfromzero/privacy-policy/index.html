Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Local Features and Game Sessions

PlentyFromZero processes template and character data, client names, technical identifiers, and template management and session records to manage character sheets and run local Bluetooth Low Energy sessions. The host receives client names and connection data and shares the session template with selected clients.

The host receives a character’s name and attributes only after the client explicitly shares that character. Saved changes are sent automatically while sharing remains active in the current session. Each character must be shared separately.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interests are providing character-sheet and multiplayer features, maintaining reliable connections, and keeping template and character data consistent.

Purchases and Free Trial

Google Play handles in-app purchases and processes payment information under its own privacy policy.

RevenueCat, Inc. processes a pseudonymous app user ID, product and transaction information, and purchase and access status on our behalf. We use this data to verify purchases and provide or restore access to PlentyFromZero Unlimited. The app may check access at startup and when you make or restore a purchase. The legal basis for processing necessary for these purposes is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

If you activate the free trial, PlentyFromZero stores and checks your trial status on your device to determine whether trial access is still available.

International Data Transfers

We transfer purchase and access data to RevenueCat, Inc. in the United States to verify purchases and provide or restore access to PlentyFromZero Unlimited. Under RevenueCat’s Data Processing Addendum, the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses apply if no applicable adequacy decision covers the transfer. If the clauses apply, you can request a copy usind the provided contact details.

Retention

Templates and characters stay on your device until you delete them. Local identifiers and template management records may remain after deletion. The host app clears temporary session data when the session ends or the app’s process terminates. Deleting data from your device does not delete copies on other participants’ devices.

Under our agreement with RevenueCat, purchase and access data are retained for the duration of the agreement, subject to RevenueCat’s backup and archiving practices.

Provision of Personal Data

To join a local session, you need a client name and connection data, but you do not need to use your real name. Without purchase verification data, we cannot activate or restore paid access to PlentyFromZero Unlimited.

Your Rights

Subject to the conditions of the GDPR, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Right to Object

You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
